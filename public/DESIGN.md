## Brand & Style
The design system articulates an Islamic academic sanctuary that bridges classical spiritual gravitas with contemporary pedagogical clarity. Designed primarily for discerning prospective parents, aspiring santri (students), and alumni, the aesthetic communicates tranquility, noble purpose, intellectual rigor, and modern institutional trust.

The visual direction rejects cluttered, overly ornamental traditionalism in favor of an academic luxury approach: vast expanses of warm breathing space, serene deep emerald greens, pristine parchment whites, and generous pillowed geometry. Classical serif typography provides intellectual nobility and sacred heritage, grounded effortlessly by pragmatic, highly legible neutral sans-serif body typography. The interaction tone is dignified, calm, and deliberate—eschewing frenetic animations for soft, fluid page transitions and restrained tactile depth.

## Colors
The palette evokes botanical lushness, academic discipline, and spiritual purity. 

- **Primary (`#198754`)**: Vibrant garden emerald representing renewal, youth, and vitality. Used for primary calls-to-action, key badges, interactive accents, and focused highlights.
- **Secondary (`#0F5132`)**: Deep nocturnal forest green carrying institutional authority, tradition, and solemnity. Used for monumental headings, header navigations, footer backgrounds, and high-contrast structural anchors.
- **Tertiary (`#2D6A4F`)**: Muted pine serving as an intermediate tone for secondary UI states, borders in colored containers, and subtle iconography.
- **Surface & Background**:
  - `surface-canvas`: `#FAFAF9` (Warm alabaster stone, reducing ocular strain while maintaining pristine warmth).
  - `surface-elevated`: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp, bright white for floating pill cards and content islands).
  - `surface-subtle`: `#F0FDF4` (Pale mint tint, reserved for highlighted quotes, key curriculum cards, and soft pill tags).
- **Neutral & Text**:
  - `text-primary`: `#18181B` (Deep ink zinc for maximum readability without the harshness of pure black).
  - `text-secondary`: `#52525B` (Balanced neutral slate for metadata, supporting body copy, and secondary guides).
  - `border-subtle`: `#E4E4E7` (Soft neutral divider lines for structured sections).
  - `border-tinted`: `#DCFCE7` (Luminescent mint border for cards resting over the warm canvas).

## Typography
The typographic architecture pairs the classical literary cadence of **EB Garamond** with the clean clarity of **Inter**. 

EB Garamond delivers poetic gravitas, moral warmth, and institutional history to all prominent display copy, value propositions, quotes, and section anchors. It should be typeset with generous leading to let letterforms breathe. 

Inter handles informational utility—curriculum breakdowns, schedules, forms, buttons, and navigation tokens. This duality separates reflective contemplation from administrative navigation, establishing immediate clarity across both mobile viewports and wide displays.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column grid system capped at a maximum width of 1280px to preserve comfortable reading line-lengths for editorial essays and program outlines.

- **Desktop (1024px and up)**: 12-column grid, 32px (`space-lg`) gutters, with minimum 48px horizontal edge margins. Section margins use expansive vertical paddings (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) to induce a calm, uncluttered temple-like environment.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column grid, 24px (`space-md`) gutters, 32px side margins. Columns condense; 3-column features reflow to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (below 768px)**: 4-column grid, 16px (`space-sm`) gutters, 16px outer margins. Vertical pacing compresses moderately to `space-xl` and `space-2xl` between sections to avoid excessive scrolling while safeguarding visual breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics sunlight diffused through courtyard awnings—gentle, expansive, and natural. Sharp drop shadows are strictly prohibited.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: Neutral surface `#FAFAF9` without shadow. Used for page-level canvas and contextual grouping backdrops.
- **Level 1 (Pill Cards & Subsections)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces layered with a composite shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)`, bordered by a gossamer hairline stroke of `#DCFCE7` or `#F4F4F5`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Dropdowns)**: Active navigation bars and focused cards: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sacred Dialogues)**: Registration wizards, admissions dialogues, and full campus tour overlays: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`, combined with a warm frosted backdrop blur (`rgba(250, 250, 249, 0.85)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).

## Shapes
Roundedness is set to level 3 (Pill-shaped & organic pillowing). The geometry references smooth river stones, archways, and soft architectural domes.

- **Buttons & Small Interactive Chips**: Fully pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Cards & Content Blocks**: `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to `rounded-3xl` (2rem / 32px), creating friendly, inviting containers.
- **Hero Banners & Visual Frames**: Large campus photography and programmatic highlight frames use an asymmetrical or uniform `rounded-3xl` (3rem / 48px) pillowed silhouette, evoking modern institutional pavilions.
- **Form Controls**: Generously rounded pill or deep-oval outlines (`border-radius: 1rem` / 16px minimum).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), solid `#198754` background, crisp white Inter text (`label-lg`), with generous horizontal padding (`px-8 py-3.5`). Hover state transitions to `#0F5132` with an ambient lift shadow.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Pill-shaped, transparent background, 1.5px border of `#0F5132` with `#0F5132` typography. Hover brings an ethereal `#F0FDF4` tint fill.
- **Ghost / Tertiary**: Undecorated pill with `#0F5132` label; transitions on hover to a subtle mint wash (`#F0FDF4`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Category Badges**: Micro-pill geometry (`rounded-full`, `px-3.5 py-1`). Set on `#F0FDF4` background with `#0F5132` text, styled with uppercase `label-sm` tracking. Optional subtle leading dot indicator colored `#198754`.

### Cards
- **Curriculum & Program Cards**: Built with `rounded-3xl` corners, `#FFFFFF` background, thin outline (`1px solid #DCFCE7`), and Level 1 elevation. Internal padding is generous (`p-8` to `p-10`). Headings use EB Garamond (`headline-md`) in `#0F5132`, paired with neutral Inter supporting copy.
- **Interactive Variant**: On hover, transitions elevate to Level 2 with a gentle border color shift toward `#198754`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs**: `rounded-2xl` containers, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E4E4E7`, height 52px, text styled with `body-md`. Focus rings bloom with a 3px diffused mint aura (`rgba(25, 135, 84, 0.15)`) and border color shifts to `#198754`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Smooth rounded-lg checkboxes (8px radius) and rounded-full radio dots. Unchecked states use soft `#E4E4E7` borders; active states fill with `#198754` displaying a crisp white micro-tick.

### Lists & Timelines
- **Academic & Daily Routine Lists**: Alternating borderless rows separated by soft hairline dividers. Bullet points are replaced with circular micro-pills containing subtle green leaf or crescent iconography, or numerals set in `label-md` inside `#F0FDF4` disks.

### Institutional Additions
- **Ayah / Quote Callout Banner**: A wide pillowed sanctuary card (`rounded-3xl`) surfaced in `#0F5132` with subtle emerald gradient waves. Classical Arabic and translation typography rendered in luminous `#F0FDF4` (EB Garamond Italic).
- **Stat Metric Islands**: Distinct floating white capsules featuring bold stats in `#198754` (`headline-xl`) paired with disciplined uppercase descriptors (`label-xs`) in `#52525B`.
